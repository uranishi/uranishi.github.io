<p class="about-profile-name">Yuki Uranishi <span class="about-profile-name__ruby">(浦西 友樹)</span></p>

Professor at the [Cybermedia Education Division, D3 Center](https://www.d3c.osaka-u.ac.jp/), [The University of Osaka](https://www.osaka-u.ac.jp/en). He also holds concurrent professorships at the [Center for Education in Liberal Arts and Sciences](https://www.celas.osaka-u.ac.jp/english/), the [School of Engineering Science](https://www.es.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/), the [Graduate School of Information Science and Technology](https://www.ist.osaka-u.ac.jp/english/), the [Center for Student Success Research and Practice](https://slics.osaka-u.ac.jp/), the [Institute for Transdisciplinary Graduate Degree Programs](https://itgp.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/), and the [Nakanoshima Art Center](https://www.art.osaka-u.ac.jp/).

He received his Ph.D. in Engineering from the [Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST)](https://www.naist.jp/en/) in 2008.
